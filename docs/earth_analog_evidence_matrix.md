# Earth Analog Evidence Matrix

## Purpose and current status

This document and the app's Evidence Matrix organize **literature-supported research candidates** by the process each may help investigate. The status for every candidate is: **Literature-supported research candidate — not yet quantitatively assessed by LUNA-TERRA.** The cited papers and agency pages are prior evidence, not measurements made by this project. LUNA-TERRA has not computationally validated these five analog relationships, selected a best site, or assessed landing-site suitability.

The candidates were selected as distinct research contexts already described in peer-reviewed studies or NASA/USGS material: Atacama for hyperaridity and soil gradients; Haughton for impact/polar exploration and operations; the McMurdo Dry Valleys for cold-desert geomorphology and ground ice; Lonar for impacts into basalt; and Río Tinto for acidic iron/sulfur geochemistry. This is a purposeful starting set, not an exhaustive inventory.

## Seven comparison dimensions

Dimensions are descriptive categories, not numeric variables or scores:

1. **Geological structure and landforms** — impact structure, stratigraphy, periglacial or aqueous landforms, and evidence for their formation.
2. **Mineralogy and geochemistry** — rock/soil mineral assemblages, major and trace elements, salts, acidity, and alteration products.
3. **Water, ice and volatile processes** — water source and phase, ground ice, sublimation, wetting/drying, and volatile retention or loss where supported by measurements.
4. **Thermal and environmental extremes** — temperature regime, aridity, radiation context, and environmental variability; the Earth atmosphere remains a fundamental difference.
5. **Terrain and surface morphology** — slope, roughness, landform scale, and spatial distribution at a stated resolution.
6. **Exploration operations and logistical constraints** — access, isolation, field safety, communications, sampling, and equipment/operations trials.
7. **Astrobiology and biosignature preservation** — habitability constraints, microbial ecology, biosignature production/preservation, and detection-method context.

Each record names only relevant dimensions. A dimension label means the literature supports a research question there; it does not mean LUNA-TERRA has measured or compared that dimension. Missing dimensions are left unclaimed.

## Candidate evidence and boundaries

|---|---|---|---|
| Atacama Desert, Chile | Selected Mars hyperaridity, soil geochemistry and biosignature detection questions | Boy et al. | Haughton Impact Crater, Devon Island | Impact geology, cold-desert/ground-ice field investigation, exploration operations and isolation | NASA Haughton-Mars Project expedition report and NASA analog-missions overview document the impact setting and field/operations research. | Earth gravity, atmosphere, hydrology, weathering and biology differ; ground ice claims need a named area and direct field evidence. |

### Lake Bonney is a separate extension

NASA's field-site description treats West Lake Bonney as an ice-covered lake relevant to **sub-ice ecosystems on icy worlds**, including under-ice chemistry and mapping. This is a supporting, lake-specific extension within the McMurdo region. It is not interchangeable with the Dry Valleys' Mars surface/permafrost analogy and is not used as a substitute for that evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy and project interpretation

The application keeps three kinds of statements separate:

- **Published/agency evidence:** descriptions and results reported by the linked peer-reviewed papers, NASA reports/pages, and USGS publication records.
- **Project interpretation:** the narrowly scoped process question LUNA-TERRA proposes to investigate for a candidate. This is qualitative synthesis, not a new scientific result.
- **Proposed future investigation:** the additional observations required before harmonized, quantitative comparison is possible.

Candidate detail pages expose the source type, link, purpose, relevant dimensions, limitations and data gaps. The evidence matrix is not a computational classification of candidate quality.

## Why there is no numerical ranking

These sites serve different planetary questions and have different types, footprints, sampling histories, spatial resolutions, seasons, units, uncertainty, and observational support. Combining those measurements before defining a target question, common variables, spatial/temporal support, normalization, missing-data rules, and uncertainty propagation would create an opaque and scientifically misleading composite. The present framework therefore does not calculate similarity, confidence percentages, an overall rank, or suitability. It does not turn a citation into a site measurement.

## Requirements for quantitative comparisons

For a defensible next stage, the project needs to define an exact AOI on each body and Earth candidate, then find compatible data with documented versions, units and uncertainty. Depending on the question, this may include:

- co-registered, quality-screened DEMs or topography at comparable effective spatial scales and a common slope/roughness method;
- geological maps, imagery and mineralogical/spectral observations with acquisition dates, resolution, calibration and detection limits;
- for water/ice questions, direct field or mission observations and process-specific measurements (e.g. ice depth/type, water activity, salinity, pH, redox, humidity or sublimation) rather than visual proxies alone;
- thermal/radiative time series and environmental context appropriate to the target process;
- field sampling metadata, laboratory methods, spatial footprints, seasonal context, and uncertainty for Earth observations;
- explicit geodetic/datum transformations and spatial registration validation for each planetary data pair;
- a predeclared question-specific comparison method, matched variables, missing-data treatment, uncertainty propagation and independent validation.

Lunar PNS02 vectors and NASA LOLA Site07 remain separate inputs in the current app. Site07 has no established frame tie to the PNS02 vectors and is not overlaid or used in these Earth candidate descriptions. No Earth datasets, site-specific environmental measurements, or cross-body comparison results are currently integrated into this matrix.
