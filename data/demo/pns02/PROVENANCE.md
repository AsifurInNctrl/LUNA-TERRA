# Demo data provenance and attribution

## Source record

- Dataset: *Complementary Data for Wueller et al. (2026): Assessing Potential Landing Sites with Favorable Illumination and Accessible, Potentially Volatile-Rich Permanently Shadowed Regions within Artemis Candidate Landing Regions*
- Authors: Lukas Wueller, Lauren M. Berger, Hephzibah Christopher, Kanori Sugimoto, Ashka Thaker, Louis Carton, William Jo, Seungil Lee, Riccardo Pedrelli, Pedro Sanchez, David A. Kring
- Version: Zenodo v1, published 2025-09-15
- DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.17084058](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17084058)
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), Zenodo API metadata `cc-by-4.0`.
- Related publication: Wueller et al. (2026), JGR: Planets 131(3), e2025JE009434, DOI [10.1029/2025JE009434](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JE009434).

## Included files

Only the essential shapefile sidecars for PSR estimates, landing-site points, and the PNS02 exploration polygons are included. No workbook, archive, raster, layer file, or unrelated location layer is packaged.

Individual SHA-256 checksums for all copied files are recorded in `checksums.csv` relative to repository root. These hashes are reproducibility checks, not substitutes for Zenodo archive-level MD5 verification. Copied files were taken from the preserved inspection workspace `LUNA-TERRA_PNS02_inspection`; originals were not altered.
